Martin Lotero Arenas WDD 231 | Individual Project Site Plan

Site name:

The Rotto Techno Collective

Purpose:

A website for DJs and techno producers in the underground scene of Pereira. The site is for artists to apply to join the collective and thus have greater access to collaborations with other artists, events, musical training, and other services.

Scenario questions:

What requirements do I need to meet to join the collective?

What benefits do I get from paying a monthly fee?

Color Scheme:

HEX #30e783 (green) for main titles

HEX #eab6ff (violet) for main paragraphs

HEX #252525 and #000 (gray and black) for backgrounds

HEX #fff and #380033 (white and dark purple) for wayfinding

Typography:

Michroma for main titles
